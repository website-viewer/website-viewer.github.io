📖 How to Create High-Converting Mockups

01

The Visual First Rule

On Instagram, the image is 90% of the ad. Ensure your media is eye-catching before users even read the caption.

02

Mobile Optimization

Most social users are on mobile. Keep your Facebook headlines short to prevent them from being cut off on smaller screens.

03

Direct CTA Match

Ensure your "Call to Action" button matches the intent of your copy. Use "Download" for lead magnets and "Shop Now" for products.

04

Branding Consistency

Use your actual brand name and logo style to see how your colors clash or complement the platform's native UI.

💡 Ad Specification Guide

Meta's advertising ecosystem uses different ratios for different placements. Use this tool to check vertical and horizontal alignments.

  • Facebook Feed: Best with 1.91:1 or 1:1 ratios. Primary text appears above the image.
  • Instagram Feed: Best with 1:1 or 4:5 ratios. The CTA button is a prominent blue/action strip below the media.
  • Text Limits: While Meta removed the 20% text rule for images, keeping text minimal in images still improves delivery.
  • Headlines: Limited to 27 characters on some mobile views before truncation occurs.
