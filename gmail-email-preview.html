💡 About Gmail Email Previews

Gmail remains the world's most popular email provider. How your email appears in the inbox—specifically the Sender Name, Subject Line, and Snippet (Preheader)—is the single biggest factor in your open rates.

  • Desktop view: Shows subject and snippet on one line. Truncation depends on screen width.
  • Mobile view: Typically stacks subject above the snippet, allowing for more vertical visibility.
  • Recommended Subject Length: Aim for 40-60 characters for maximum compatibility.
  • The "Secret" Preheader: If you don't define a preheader, Gmail will pull the first text it finds in your email (like "View in browser" or navigation links).
  • Icons: Stars and attachment paperclips take up horizontal space and can impact how much text is shown.
