📖 How to Influence the Knowledge Graph

01

Structured Data

Implement Organization or Person schema markup on your homepage. This is the primary way to "talk" to Google's Knowledge Graph.

02

Wikipedia & Wikidata

Google heavily relies on neutral third-party sources. Having a Wikipedia or Wikidata entry is the most consistent way to trigger a panel.

03

Verify Your Entity

Once a panel appears, click "Claim this knowledge panel" to suggest edits and verify your official social media profiles.

04

Consistent NAP

Ensure your Name, Address, and Phone (NAP) are identical across Google Business Profile, LinkedIn, and your website.

💡 Knowledge Panel Specifications

The Knowledge Graph is an entity-based system. It doesn't just rank pages; it understands real-world relationships between things.

  • Main Image: Google prefers square logos or high-quality landscape photos for organizations.
  • Social Icons: These are pulled automatically based on sameAs properties in your Schema markup.
  • Sourcing: Descriptions are usually truncated at ~250 characters before a "Wikipedia" or "Official Website" link.
  • Local vs. Brand: Brand panels (shown here) are for global entities. Local panels include maps and reviews.
