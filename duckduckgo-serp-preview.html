💡 About DuckDuckGo Search

DuckDuckGo is a privacy-first search engine that does not track or profile users. It has grown rapidly to serve hundreds of millions of searches per month and is the default browser on iOS in many regions. For SEO purposes, DuckDuckGo sources its organic results primarily from Bing's index, supplemented by its own crawl of niche content and over 400 other sources.

A standout DDG feature is its Instant Answer boxes — pulled from Wikipedia, Wolfram Alpha, and other trusted open sources — which can appear above organic results and may capture clicks that never reach your page (zero-click searches).

  • DDG uses the same core index as Bing — Bing Webmaster Tools optimisation benefits DDG automatically
  • Title display: approximately 63 characters before truncation
  • Description: DDG typically shows around 120–145 characters on desktop
  • DDG provides DuckDuckHack for contributing to Instant Answers
  • Schema.org structured data is read and used for rich formatting — same markup as for Bing/Google
  • DDG respects robots.txt and the noindex meta tag via its own DuckDuckBot crawler
  • DDG's audience skews toward privacy-conscious, tech-savvy users — high-quality content and HTTPS are especially important
