How to Use the Pinterest Pin Preview Tool

1

Upload Your Pin Image

Click the upload area and select your pin image. Pinterest supports various aspect ratios — choose the one that matches your image using the ratio selector.

2

Select an Aspect Ratio

Pinterest recommends 2:3 (1000×1500px) for most pins, but also supports 1:1, 4:5, and 9:16. Selecting the right ratio helps the preview match your actual pin dimensions.

3

Write Title and Description

Enter your pin title and a compelling description. Both appear below the image in the feed and are important for both engagement and Pinterest SEO.

4

Set Username and Board Name

Add your Pinterest username and the board the pin belongs to. These show in the card footer exactly as on the live platform.

5

Hover to See Save Button

Hover over the preview card to see the Save button overlay — the same red overlay that appears when users hover over a pin in the Pinterest feed.

About This Tool

The Pinterest Pin Preview Tool simulates the Pinterest feed pin card so you can validate your visual content and copy before creating an account post.

Pinterest is a highly visual platform where the image quality, aspect ratio, and title are the primary drivers of saves and engagement. This tool lets content creators, bloggers, and e-commerce brands ensure their pin will stand out before it goes live.
