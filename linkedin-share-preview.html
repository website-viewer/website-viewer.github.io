💡 About LinkedIn Link Sharing

LinkedIn renders link cards when a URL is included in a post or message. Like Facebook and Twitter, LinkedIn reads OpenGraph meta tags to build these previews. However, LinkedIn applies its own rendering rules and has stricter image requirements than other platforms.

LinkedIn is particularly important for B2B content. A well-crafted link card on LinkedIn can drive significant traffic from a professional audience — making OG optimization directly tied to business outcomes.

  • Recommended image size: 1200×627px (LinkedIn-specific ratio)
  • Minimum image size: 200×200px — smaller images are ignored
  • LinkedIn's crawler respects og:title, og:description, og:image, and og:url
  • After updating tags, use the LinkedIn Post Inspector to force a cache refresh
  • Titles over 150 characters are truncated in the feed view
  • LinkedIn penalizes outbound link posts in organic reach — a strong image and title helps overcome this
