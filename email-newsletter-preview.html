💡 Why Preview Your Newsletter?

In a crowded inbox, you only have a split second to capture attention. Most users decide whether to open an email based solely on the Subject Line and Preheader. If these elements are cut off by the email client, your message might be ignored or deleted.

  • The Subject Line: Most desktop clients show about 60 characters, while mobile clients may only show 30-40.
  • The Preheader: This is the "snippet" text that follows the subject. If you don't define it, clients will pull the first text from your email (often "View in browser"), which looks unprofessional.
  • Sender Recognition: Ensure your sender name is recognizable and consistent to build trust with your audience.
  • Mobile Responsiveness: Over 50% of emails are opened on mobile devices. Testing your content layout in a narrow view is essential.
