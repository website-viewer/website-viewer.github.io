💡 About Outlook Email Previews

Outlook is a staple in B2B environments. Unlike Gmail's single-line desktop layout, Outlook's "Focused" and "Other" tabs often display emails in a list view that prioritizes the Sender and Subject vertically.

  • Desktop Layout: Outlook's standard layout shows the subject on the second line and the snippet on the third line, though this varies by user configuration.
  • Character Limits: While subjects can be long, most users only see the first 35-45 characters before truncation on mobile.
  • Unread Indicators: Outlook uses a distinctive blue vertical bar on the left to indicate unread messages.
  • Flagging: Flags (equivalent to stars) appear on the right and can change the visual weight of the email entry.
  • The Snippet: Outlook pulls the first plain text it finds. Using our tool ensures your hidden preheader text looks professional.
