📖 How to Optimize LinkedIn Ads

01

The "See More" Break

Introductory text is truncated after ~150 characters on mobile. Ensure your primary value proposition is in the first sentence.

02

70-Character Headlines

Keep headlines concise. If they exceed 70 characters, they may be truncated, especially when paired with a long CTA button.

03

Professional Imagery

LinkedIn users respond well to high-quality photography, infographics, or clean, branded illustrations that look "corporate" yet modern.

04

Contextual CTAs

"Download" works exceptionally well for lead magnets (Whitepapers/Ebooks), while "Register" is the standard for Webinars.

💡 LinkedIn Ad Specifications

LinkedIn Single Image Ads are the workhorse of B2B marketing. They appear directly in the feed of decision-makers and professionals.

  • Introductory Text: Up to 600 characters, but 150 or fewer is recommended for better mobile performance.
  • Headline: 70 characters max to avoid truncation.
  • Image Ratio: 1.91:1 (Desktop/Mobile) or 1:1 (Square). 1200 x 627 pixels is the standard recommendation.
  • Audience Tone: Unlike TikTok or Facebook, LinkedIn audiences prefer a professional, educational, or industry-focused tone.
