💡 About Yahoo Search

Yahoo Search is powered by Microsoft Bing's index under a long-standing partnership, meaning optimising for Bing effectively optimises for Yahoo as well. Despite sharing an index, Yahoo renders its own SERP design with the familiar purple colour scheme, distinct URL formatting, and its own layout for rich results and news.

Yahoo still commands a meaningful share of desktop search traffic in the United States, particularly among older demographics and users of the Yahoo Mail and Yahoo News ecosystems. A well-optimised snippet that performs in Bing will inherently surface on Yahoo.

  • Title display: Yahoo shows approximately 65 characters before truncation
  • Description: Yahoo displays around 140–155 characters of the meta description
  • Yahoo inherits Bing's crawl data — submit your sitemap to Bing Webmaster Tools and it will propagate to Yahoo
  • Yahoo frequently pulls news results from its own editorial sources; schema.org Article markup helps eligibility
  • Structured data supported: Review, Product, Recipe, Article, Event, FAQ and more
  • Yahoo Search does not have its own webmaster console — use Bing Webmaster Tools for indexing and insights
