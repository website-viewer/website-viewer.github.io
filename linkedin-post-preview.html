How to Use the LinkedIn Post Preview Tool

1

Enter Your Name and Headline

Type your LinkedIn display name and professional headline — these appear in the post header just as they do on the real platform.

2

Write Your Post Content

Paste your full LinkedIn post into the text area. Line breaks, emojis, arrows (→), and hashtags are all supported and render accurately.

3

Add an Optional Image

Upload a photo, infographic, or banner to simulate a post with media — common for engagement-driving LinkedIn content.

4

Toggle LinkedIn Premium

Switch on the Premium badge if you have a Premium account. This adds the gold star icon next to your name.

5

Set Reactions, Comments & Reposts

Use sliders to dial in engagement numbers. The total appears in the reactions bar with stacked emoji icons, just like the real feed.

About This Tool

The LinkedIn Post Preview Tool simulates the current LinkedIn feed post card so you can check formatting, review copy, and present content to clients or stakeholders before publication.

LinkedIn has specific quirks — long posts are truncated with a "See more" button, line breaks matter greatly for readability, and emoji use is common despite the professional context. This tool helps you nail all of those details before you go live.
