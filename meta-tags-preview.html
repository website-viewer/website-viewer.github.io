📖 Why Meta Tags Matter for SEO

01

Click-Through Rate

Meta titles and descriptions are your "sales pitch" in search results. A well-optimized snippet can increase organic clicks significantly.

02

Social Engagement

Open Graph (OG) tags control how your links look on social media. Without them, platforms might pull irrelevant images or text from your site.

03

Search Ranking

While meta descriptions aren't a direct ranking factor, the CTR they generate tells Google your page is relevant to the user's query.

04

Branding

Consistent meta tags ensure that your brand voice and visual identity are preserved across Google, Facebook, X, and LinkedIn.

💡 Character Limit Best Practices

  • Title Tags: Keep between 50-60 characters. This ensures the title isn't cut off in search results on most devices.
  • Meta Descriptions: Aim for 120-155 characters. This range provides enough detail to be useful while remaining fully visible.
  • OG Images: Use 1200 x 630 pixels. This aspect ratio (1.91:1) is the standard for high-resolution previews on social feeds.
  • Canonical URL: Always include a canonical tag to prevent duplicate content issues if your page can be accessed via multiple URLs.
