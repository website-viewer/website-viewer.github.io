📖 How to Optimize Facebook Ads

01

The "Three Line" Rule

On mobile, Facebook truncates primary text after 3 lines. Put your most important hook or offer at the very beginning.

02

Eye-Catching Visuals

Use high-contrast images. Square (1:1) or Vertical (4:5) ratios occupy the most screen real estate in the news feed.

03

Strong Headlines

Keep headlines under 40 characters. Longer headlines may be cut off, especially on smaller mobile devices.

04

Relevant CTAs

Match your button to your goal. "Shop Now" works best for e-commerce, while "Learn More" is better for high-ticket services.

💡 Meta Ad Specs & Best Practices

Meta (Facebook & Instagram) ads are highly visual. Our preview tool helps you ensure that your text and images work together without being cluttered.

  • Aspect Ratio: 1:1 (Square) is recommended for Feed ads. 9:16 is recommended for Stories and Reels.
  • Text Overlay: While the "20% text rule" is no longer strictly enforced, ads with less text on the image itself generally perform better.
  • Character Limits: Primary Text: 125 characters (recommended); Headline: 40 characters; Description: 30 characters.
