💡 About Twitter Cards

Twitter Cards are meta tags that allow you to attach rich media experiences to tweets that link to your content. When someone tweets a link to your website, Twitter reads these tags and renders a card directly in the tweet — boosting visibility and click-through dramatically compared to plain link tweets.

There are two main card types in common use today: Summary shows a small thumbnail beside your title and description, while Summary Large Image renders a full-width hero image above your content — this is the most impactful format for engagement.

Important rules:

  • Your image must be at least 600×314px (1200×628px recommended) for large cards
  • Title is capped at 70 characters before truncation
  • Description is shown up to 200 characters
  • Card images must be under 5MB in file size
  • Use twitter:site with your @handle to get attribution
  • Twitter validates cards when a link is first shared — use the Twitter Card Validator to confirm after deployment
