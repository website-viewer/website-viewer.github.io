How to Use the Snapchat Story Preview Tool

1

Enter Your Username

Type your Snapchat username. It appears at the top of the story viewer alongside the avatar — just like the real story screen.

2

Upload a Story Background

Upload the image you plan to share as your story background. It fills the phone screen as a full-bleed image behind your overlays.

3

Add Text Overlay

Enter your story text and choose from 5 text style presets — white on dark, yellow, red background, blue background, or transparent — to match your visual.

4

Add Stickers

Click any emoji in the sticker grid to add it to your story. Multiple stickers can be added to simulate a fully decorated snap. Use the Clear button to reset them.

5

Set View Count and Age

Set your story view count with the slider and edit the story age (2h, 10m, etc.) to simulate how the story looks at different points in its 24-hour lifespan.

About This Tool

The Snapchat Story Preview Tool renders a realistic Snapchat story viewer inside a phone frame — complete with the progress bar, profile header, text overlay, stickers, reply bar, and view counter.

Brand marketers, influencers, and social media managers use this tool to plan Story content, validate text overlay placement, and present story mock-ups to clients before designing the final visual asset in a dedicated design tool.
