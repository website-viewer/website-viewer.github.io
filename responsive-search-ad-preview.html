💡 Understanding Responsive Search Ads

Responsive Search Ads (RSA) are now the default ad type for Google Search campaigns. Unlike the older "Expanded Text Ads," RSAs allow you to provide multiple versions of headlines and descriptions.

  • Dynamic Selection: Google automatically tests different combinations and learns which ones perform best for different search queries.
  • Ad Strength: This is a metric used by Google to measure the quantity and diversity of your ad assets. High diversity usually leads to better ad strength.
  • Flexibility: Because the ad size adapts to the user's device, your headlines might be shown in different orders or even omitted to fit the screen.
