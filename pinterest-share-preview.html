💡 About Pinterest Link Sharing

Pinterest is a visual discovery platform where users save ("pin") content from across the web to curated boards. When someone pins your URL, Pinterest uses OpenGraph tags and Pinterest-specific meta tags to populate the pin's title, description, and image. Visually optimized pins drive significant referral traffic — Pinterest is one of the top sources of social referral traffic for lifestyle, food, fashion, and home content.

Unlike other social platforms, Pinterest pins have a long lifespan — a well-optimized pin can drive traffic for months or even years after being shared.

  • Best image ratio: 2:3 (e.g., 1000×1500px) — tall images perform better in the Pinterest feed
  • Add <meta name="pinterest-rich-pin" content="true"> to enable Rich Pins with extra metadata
  • Use og:image with a high-resolution image — Pinterest downscales but benefits from quality sources
  • Include descriptive alt attributes on all images for Pinterest's visual search
  • Pinterest supports Article, Product, and Recipe Rich Pin types with extra structured data
  • Apply to be a verified merchant or site to unlock enhanced Rich Pin capabilities
