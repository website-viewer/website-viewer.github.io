💡 About Apple Mail Previews

Apple Mail remains one of the most popular email clients globally due to its integration with iPhone and Mac. Understanding its unique rendering logic is key for email marketers.

  • The Unread Dot: Apple uses a bright blue dot. It is positioned to the left of the content and shifts the entire text block.
  • Snippet Length: Unlike Gmail, Apple Mail is generous with snippet text, often showing two full lines on iOS and macOS.
  • Desktop Hierarchy: On Mac, the Subject line is bold while the Sender is regular weight. This is the opposite of the iOS mobile layout.
  • Font Rendering: Apple uses the system font (San Francisco), which is highly legible even at small sizes, allowing for longer previews than many competitors.
