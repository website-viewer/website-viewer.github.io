📖 Optimizing TikTok Ads

01

The Safe Zone

Avoid placing text or logos on the right side or the bottom 25% of the screen. The TikTok UI elements will cover them.

02

The First 3 Seconds

TikTok is fast. Your visual hook must happen immediately to stop users from swiping past your ad.

03

Caption Strategy

Keep captions brief. While you have 150 characters, only the first 2 lines are visible before being hidden under "more."

04

Native Look

Ads that look like organic TikToks (shot on a phone, using trending sounds) generally have higher engagement than high-production commercials.

💡 TikTok In-Feed Ad Specs

In-Feed ads are the standard video format that appears as users scroll through their "For You" feed. They are immersive, full-screen, and sound-on by default.

  • Aspect Ratio: Strictly 9:16 (1080x1920 pixels). Using other ratios will result in black bars and lower performance.
  • Video Length: 5 to 60 seconds (9-15 seconds is the "sweet spot" for performance).
  • CTA Button: The button color is dynamically chosen by TikTok but is usually high-contrast (Red or White).
  • Interaction: Users can like, comment, and share just like an organic video, which provides social proof for your brand.
