💡 About OpenGraph Tags

OpenGraph (OG) is an internet protocol created by Facebook that allows any web page to become a rich object in a social graph. When you share a URL on Facebook, LinkedIn, Discord, Slack, or most messaging apps, the platform reads your page's OG meta tags to generate a rich preview card — complete with title, description, and image.

Without proper OG tags, platforms fall back to guessing your content, often producing ugly or misleading previews. Correct OG metadata can dramatically increase click-through rates and engagement when your links are shared.

Key tags to include:

  • og:title — The title shown in the card (ideally 60–90 characters)
  • og:description — A concise summary (ideally 120–160 characters)
  • og:image — Your card image; use at least 1200×630px for best results
  • og:url — The canonical URL of your page
  • og:type — Content type: website, article, product, etc.
  • og:site_name — Your brand or website name
