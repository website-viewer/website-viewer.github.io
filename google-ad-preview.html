💡 Google Ads Optimization Tips

Creating a high-converting Google Ad requires more than just filling in the boxes. Here are industry best practices for Search Ads:

  • Keyword Relevance: Ensure your Headline 1 contains the primary keyword the user searched for. This increases your Quality Score and CTR.
  • Benefit-Driven Copy: Instead of just listing features, tell the user what they gain (e.g., "Save 50%" instead of "Big Sale").
  • The 30-30-90 Rule: Google allows 30 characters for headlines and 90 for descriptions. Use as much of this space as possible without making the ad look cluttered.
  • Visual Hierarchy: The headlines are the most important part of the ad. Make sure Headline 1 is your strongest hook.
