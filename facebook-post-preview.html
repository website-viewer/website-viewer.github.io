How to Use the Facebook Post Preview Tool

1

Set Your Author Name and Type

Enter your name or Facebook Page name. Switch between a personal profile and a Page using the dropdown — this controls whether the "Page" badge appears next to your name.

2

Write Your Post Text

Type or paste your post content into the text area. Emojis and line breaks are supported and will render accurately in the preview card below.

3

Optionally Add an Image

Upload a photo that will appear below your post text, just like a standard photo post on the Facebook news feed.

4

Set Visibility and Time

Choose who the post is "visible" to (Public, Friends, Only me) and set how long ago it was posted to complete the authentic look.

5

Adjust Reactions, Comments & Shares

Use the sliders to dial in realistic engagement numbers. Likes and Love reactions are summed into a total reaction count automatically.

About This Tool

The Facebook Post Preview Tool generates a realistic news feed mock-up so you can review your content before publishing. It mirrors the current Facebook post card design including the author header, reaction bar, and action buttons.

Social media managers, content teams, and agencies use this tool to get client approval on copy, check emoji rendering, and validate that the post structure looks correct — all without needing to actually publish to Facebook.
