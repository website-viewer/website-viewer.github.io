How to Use the TikTok Video Preview Tool

1

Set Your Username

Enter your TikTok @handle. This appears at the bottom of the video overlay and on the avatar button in the sidebar.

2

Upload a Video Thumbnail

Upload any image or video screenshot to use as the video background. This fills the phone screen exactly as a real TikTok would look.

3

Write Your Caption and Hashtags

Add your caption text and hashtags in separate fields. Both appear at the bottom of the video in the native TikTok overlay style.

4

Set Sound Name

Enter the name of the sound or original audio for your video. The spinning disc animation with sound name is shown at the bottom.

5

Adjust Engagement Numbers

Use the sliders to set likes, comments, saves, and shares on the right sidebar — exactly how they appear on the TikTok For You Page.

About This Tool

The TikTok Video Preview Tool renders a realistic TikTok For You Page experience inside a phone frame — including the overlay UI, right sidebar action buttons, and bottom navigation bar.

TikTok creators, brand managers, and agencies use this tool to preview how captions, hashtags, and thumbnails will look before posting. It's especially useful for planning content series or presenting video concepts to clients.
