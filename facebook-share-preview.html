💡 About Facebook Link Sharing

When a URL is shared on Facebook — whether in a post, a comment, or via Messenger — Facebook's crawler (facebookexternalhit) fetches your page and reads its OpenGraph meta tags to build the link preview. The quality of your OG tags has a direct impact on engagement: posts with rich previews consistently outperform plain-URL posts in clicks and shares.

Facebook renders link cards in different layouts depending on context. The large image card (News Feed default) shows a full-width image above your domain, title, and description. The thumbnail card appears when sharing to specific groups or when no large image is detected. Messenger shows a compact card inside the chat bubble.

Facebook-specific tips:

  • Image dimensions: 1200×630px for large cards, 200×200px minimum for thumbnail
  • Facebook caches OG data aggressively — use the Sharing Debugger to force a refresh after changes
  • Title should be concise: Facebook truncates at ~65 characters in most views
  • Include og:url to prevent Facebook from choosing a random canonical URL
  • Avoid misleading previews — Facebook penalizes clickbait with reduced distribution
