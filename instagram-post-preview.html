How to Use the Instagram Post Preview Tool

1

Enter Your Profile Details

Type your Instagram username, display name, and optional location tag in the left panel. These appear exactly as they would in the post header.

2

Upload Your Post Image

Click the image upload area or drag and drop a photo. Supports JPG, PNG, and GIF. Square images (1:1) are recommended for a true Instagram look.

3

Write Your Caption & Hashtags

Add your caption text and hashtags in the respective fields. Hashtags are highlighted in blue automatically in the preview, just like the real app.

4

Adjust Engagement Numbers

Use the sliders to set realistic-looking like and comment counts. This helps you visualise how popular posts appear on the feed.

5

Review and Screenshot

Once satisfied, take a screenshot of the preview card to share with your team, client, or for content planning purposes.

About This Tool

The Instagram Post Preview Tool lets you create a pixel-accurate mock-up of an Instagram feed post directly in your browser — no account needed, no data sent to any server.

Content creators, social media managers, and marketing agencies use this tool to plan and approve posts before they go live. By previewing the full composition — image, caption, hashtags, and engagement stats — you can catch formatting issues, caption typos, or hashtag errors before they reach your audience.

The tool simulates the real Instagram web interface including the gradient story ring around the avatar, the action bar with heart, comment, share and save icons, and the accurate typography and spacing used by Instagram.
